What Wapsi does03 / 12

Trace. Hold the amount. Verify. A human signs.

1

Trace

Follow the complaint's money through every layer of mule accounts, split per victim pro-rata (SOP 5(xiii)).

transactions · NCRP text · voice notes
2

Amount-specific hold

Hold min(victim money, balance) per account. Never a whole-account freeze unless an approved, recorded exception.

SOP 5(ix), 9.1
3

Verified drafts

STR, hold instructions, customer notice, restoration packet. Gemma 4 writes the words; the verifier recomputes every number and citation.

numbers from data, words from the model

Maker-checker

How predictions work05 / 12

Transparent predictions. Never the money.

Predicted: the account's role

Mule, innocent holder, merchant, pool account. A transparent logistic scorer (default) with per-feature reasons; a GraphSAGE GNN on ROCm behind the same interface.

Never predicted: the amount

A hold is arithmetic, min(victim money, balance). Score ≥ 0.6 mule, ≤ 0.35 innocent, between: the officer decides.

Hard rules first

A merchant needs a delivered invoice for every disputed credit. Pool accounts never get a forward hold.

Google partner technology09 / 12

Gemma 4 writes. Google ADK runs the agent.

Gemma 4 31B

  • Primary model for draft prose, complaint and voice-note fields
  • Never types a number: placeholders only
  • Every draft names the model that wrote it

Google ADK agent

  • 10 pipeline tools, native tool calls from Gemma 4
  • JSON tool protocol as the safety net
  • A reply with an amount no tool returned is replaced

Agent trace

  • Every tool call with guard and verifier verdicts
  • Redacted and sealed into the case's audit chain
  • Replay is always labelled as a recording

Honest limits and next steps11 / 12

What this is not, yet.

Limits we state

  • All banking data is synthetic. No real customer, victim or bank data.
  • CFCFRMS is a simulated interface; nothing is sent to the portal.
  • The RBI money-mule SOP is a DRAFT (comments closed 2 Oct 2026), never treated as in force.
  • GNN validation AUC 1.0 is on a saturated world; the heuristic stays the default.
  • Our metric is money identified and held correctly, not money restored.

Next

  • A pilot with one bank's fraud desk on data it controls.
  • Payment aggregators whose pool accounts get frozen.
  • Voice intake with Whisper in the loop, measured end to end.
  • A verifier-filtered LoRA of Gemma 4 on the MI300X.
  • Price set after bank validation calls.
