Who

The officer who acts on cyber-fraud complaints from the 1930 helpline (NCRP).

Does in Wapsi

Opens a complaint and follows the money, hop by hop.

Gets

A hold plan: the exact amount to hold in each account, and the SOP rule behind it.

Gets

Use case 4 of 7
The police

Who

Investigating officers who ask the bank to hold money.

Does in Wapsi

The bank checks their hold request against an eight-field checklist.

Gets

A hold on the amount that can be traced, and a letter listing what is missing.

Use case 5 of 7
Compliance and audit

Who

Compliance teams, internal auditors, the regulator.

Does in Wapsi

Looks back at any case: who did what, when, and in which role.

Gets

A tamper-evident log, and deadline clocks that count down by themselves.
